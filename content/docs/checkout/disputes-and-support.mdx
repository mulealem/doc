# Disputes and support

Two escape hatches are available on the hosted checkout page:

## Disputes

If a customer's payment was rejected (e.g. the OCR misread the amount, or the receipt photo was blurry), they can open a **dispute** from the checkout page. This creates a `Claim` row in PyGate and notifies you.

Disputes are visible in the dashboard under **Project → Disputes**. From there you can:

- View the receipt and the OCR output
- Manually approve (which re-runs the verification) or reject
- Reply to the customer (their email is captured)
- Mark as resolved

When a dispute is resolved, a `dispute.resolved` webhook is delivered (if you're subscribed to that event).

## Support tickets

For anything that isn't a payment dispute, the customer can open a **support ticket** from the checkout page footer. This creates a `SupportTicket` row and emails your team's support inbox (configured in Project → Settings).

Tickets are not tied to a specific order or payment — they're a generic contact channel. You can reply by email or close them from the dashboard.

## Programmatic access

Today, disputes and support tickets are dashboard-only. A read-only API:

```
GET /api/v1/disputes
GET /api/v1/support-tickets
```

is in the roadmap for v1.5. Until then, if you need to integrate disputes into your own internal tooling, use the [webhooks](/webhooks/events) (`order.disputed`, `dispute.resolved`).

<Callout type="info">
  The customer's email is the only piece of PII the checkout page collects. It's not stored beyond the dispute/ticket lifetime unless you've explicitly enabled customer history in the dashboard.
</Callout>
